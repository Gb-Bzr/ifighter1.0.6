*Movelist 
===================================================
Double Lariat: Any 2 punches

Quick Double Lariat: Any 2 kicks
     
Banishing Flat: F, D, DF, any punch
   
Aerial Russian Slam: DP, any kick       
    
Screw Pile Driver: 360° + any punch       
      
Flying Powerbomb: HCF, any kick(far)      
        
Atomic Suplex: HCF, any kick(close)            
       
Iron Claw: DF, MP or FP                     

Dash Tsukami Nage: F, F             

Backdrop (Instant Backdrop): FP or hold FP (during grab from Dash Tsukami Nage)

Kamitsuki (Instant Kamitsuki): FK or hold FK  (during grab from Dash Tsukami Nage)

Body Press: D, FP in air                  

Kuuchuu Headbutt: U, MP in air       

Double Knee Drop: D, MK in air           

Mid-Air Elbow Drop: D, MP in air         

Elbow Drop: F, MP on ground                 

Final Atomic Buster: 360° + any 2 punches (Need Level 1)

Iron Body: B, D, BD, LK (Need Level 1)

Ultra Final Atomic Buster: 360° + any 2 kicks (Need Level 3)
